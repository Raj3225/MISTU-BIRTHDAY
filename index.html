My dear Mistu,

There are so many things I love about you, but your eyes are something I can never stop noticing. ❤️

And more than that, I love the way you behave, the way you care, and especially the way you console me whenever I'm not okay.

Sometimes you probably don't even realize how much those little things mean to me.

One memory I will always keep close to my heart is that day at Rabindra Sarobar, sitting together in the rain and just spending time with each other. 🌧️❤️

It wasn't something complicated or extraordinary, but being there with you made that moment incredibly special for me.

On your birthday, I don't just wish you happiness for today.

I wish you a great future, good health, peace, success, and countless beautiful moments. And selfishly, I hope I get to be beside you through many of those moments. 🥺❤️

I want us to grow together, experience life together, laugh together, fight less, understand each other more, and create many more memories like that rainy day at Rabindra Sarobar.

Thank you for being you, Mistu.

You are a very special part of my life, and I hope you always remember that.

Happy Birthday, my Mistu. 🎂❤️

With all my love,
— Raj ❤️
